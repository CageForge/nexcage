# Governance

nexcage has one active maintainer ([MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)), who
decides:

- Day-to-day: on the pull request, reviewed as MAINTAINERS.md says.
- Architectural: an ADR in `docs/architecture/`, discussed on its pull request.
- Releases: the maintainer tags them and writes the release notes.

With two or more active maintainers, an architectural change needs the approval
of a maintainer other than its author, and a dispute is settled by an ADR and a
majority of maintainers.
